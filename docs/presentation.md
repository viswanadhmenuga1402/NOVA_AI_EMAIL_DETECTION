\# NOVA AI - Project Presentation



\# Problem Statement



Spam and phishing emails are among the most common cyber threats. Traditional filters often fail to explain why an email is dangerous.



\---



\# Proposed Solution



NOVA AI combines Machine Learning with rule-based security analysis to accurately detect malicious emails and provide explainable results.



\---



\# Objectives



\- Detect spam emails

\- Detect phishing attacks

\- Analyze sender reputation

\- Calculate overall risk

\- Explain detection decisions

\- Maintain scan history

\- Generate reports
